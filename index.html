Faith Leskowitz Artist

Faith Leskowitz is a Houston-based artist and painter specializing in expressive, impressionistic floral paintings, colorful landscapes, and custom portrait artwork. Working primarily in acrylic, she creates vibrant, layered pieces inspired by nature, light, and everyday beauty. Her original paintings and fine art prints are collected by homeowners, art enthusiasts, and gift buyers looking for colorful contemporary artwork that brings warmth and personality to any space.
